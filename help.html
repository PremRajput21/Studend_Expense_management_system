How can we help?

Quick answers to common questions about your student expense tracker.

How do I add an expense?

Choose Add expense from the sidebar, fill in the name, amount, category, and date, then select Save expense.

Where is my data stored?

This is a front-end learning project. It stores expenses in your browser's local storage. Data does not automatically sync to another device.

How do I set my monthly budget?

Open My budget, enter your monthly limit in rupees, and click Save budget. The dashboard will update automatically.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Visit Reports or Settings and use Export CSV to download a spreadsheet-friendly copy of your expenses.

Is the login secure?

No. This demonstration stores account information in local browser storage and is not suitable for real users or sensitive information. A production system needs server-side authentication, password hashing, and a database.
